## What hair problems are there
Hair loss and excessive hair growth are frequent reasons for a consultation. These disorders can not only significantly impair wellbeing, but can also be symptoms of various underlying conditions.

## How do they develop
## The causes of hair growth disorders include
- Hormonal disorders

- Deficiencies of various trace elements and vitamins

- Side effects of medication

- Genetic factors

## What can we do about them
## Our first priority is to investigate the causes
- Macroscopic, microscopic and computer-assisted examination of the hair root

- Blood analyses

## Once the diagnosis has been confirmed, treatment follows
- Local treatments with solutions and ointments

- Oral medication

- Laser treatment

- Hair removal (IPL epilation)

- Mesotherapy

- Hair transplants
