At our practice, we understand the importance of beautiful skin. That is why we are proud to offer the latest technology in skincare – the Aquafacial treatment.

Aquafacial is a non-invasive treatment that uses a combination of water, antioxidants and serum to deeply cleanse, exfoliate and renew the skin. The treatment is gentle yet highly effective and can be tailored to address a wide range of skin concerns. It is painless and requires no recovery time.

## The benefits of Aquafacial are
- Improved skin tone and texture

- Reduction of fine lines and wrinkles

- Fewer acne breakouts

- Improved hydration

- Increased collagen production

- Reduction of skin roughness (keratosis pilaris)

In addition, Aquafacial is suitable for all skin types and can be performed on the face and body.
