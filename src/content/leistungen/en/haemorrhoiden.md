## What are haemorrhoids
Haemorrhoids are among the very common disorders of the anal (rectal) region: small nodules located in the transitional area between the rectum and the anal canal. In the early stages they often cause no major complaints.

## Signs include
- Blood in the stool

- Itching in the anal area

- Pain during bowel movements

- Mucous discharge

- Moisture

- Anal eczema

## How do they develop
Haemorrhoids develop as a result of enlarged veins in the rectal and anal area.

## Contributing factors include
- A low-fibre diet

- Insufficient fluid intake

- Sedentary work

- Being overweight

- Lack of exercise

- Constipation

## What can we do about it
First, depending on the individual case, we carry out various examinations:

- Stool diagnostics

- Digital rectal examination

- Proctoscopy (examination of the anal canal)

- Allergy testing

We then initiate a therapy individually tailored to you, such as:

- Injections

- Ligation of the nodules

- Surgery

The methods are mostly painless or involve very little pain, so there is no impairment to your everyday life.
