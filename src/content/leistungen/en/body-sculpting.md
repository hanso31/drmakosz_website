## What is 3D lipomed
The system combines cryolipolysis, radiofrequency, cavitation and vacuum – lipomassage. For the first time, this innovative non-invasive treatment makes it possible to reach different layers of the skin simultaneously and tighten them in parallel.

## What are the indications
- Cellulite

- Body contouring / fat reduction

- Stretch marks

- Skin tightening

- Lymphatic drainage

We are happy to discuss the options with you during a consultation.
