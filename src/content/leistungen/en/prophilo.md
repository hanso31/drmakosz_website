Profhilo® is an innovative injectable treatment developed to combat the signs of ageing such as wrinkles and loss of skin elasticity. It consists of hyaluronic acid, a substance that occurs naturally in the body and is responsible for maintaining skin hydration and elasticity.

What distinguishes Profhilo® from other injectable treatments is that it improves skin quality and reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles by stimulating the production of collagen and elastin.

Profhilo® is a painless, non-surgical treatment. You will notice an immediate improvement in skin hydration and texture, with full results becoming visible after a few weeks.

Profhilo® is suitable for all skin types and age groups, making it an ideal solution for anyone who wants to feel and look good.
