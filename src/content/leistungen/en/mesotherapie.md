## What is mesotherapy
It is a highly effective, gentle treatment method developed by the French physician Michel Pistor. It incorporates elements of acupuncture, neural therapy and the reflex zones of the skin. Injections of vitamins and trace elements are delivered into the middle (meso) layer of the skin.

## What we can achieve with mesotherapy
- Skin tightening

- Skin rejuvenation

- Improvement of the complexion

- Cellulite reduction

- Fat reduction
