## What is laser therapy
A laser is a device that produces coherent light. The term is used both for the light source and for the light beam itself.

Through the targeted application of light waves, layers of skin can be ablated and blood vessels broken down.

Laser medicine and IPL systems have developed rapidly in recent years. With various laser systems, we can treat a great many skin conditions with few side effects and little pain. Lasers have now become an indispensable part of modern dermatological therapy.

## What we can achieve with laser therapy
- Mole removal

- Fibroma removal

- Xanthelasma removal

- Pigment reduction

- Skin tightening

- Skin rejuvenation

- Improvement of the complexion

- Spider vein treatment
