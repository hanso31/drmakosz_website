## What are pigmentation disorders
Brownish or whitish shifts in skin pigmentation, very common on sun-exposed areas such as the face and décolleté. These include, for example, age spots, freckles or brownish pigmented scars.

## How do they develop
They arise from irregular accumulations of pigment in the skin and are often intensified – or even caused – by sun exposure.

## What can we do about them
First, we examine the pigmentation disorder very closely with a dermatoscope and make the appropriate diagnosis.

## Available treatment options are
- Laser therapy

- Chemical peels

- Photodynamic therapy

- Ointment treatments
