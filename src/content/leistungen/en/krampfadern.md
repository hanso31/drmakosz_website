## What are varicose veins
Varicose vein disease (also known as varicosis) manifests itself through enlargement of the superficial veins and weakness of the venous valves.

Spider veins represent the mildest form of these changes. They are a frequent cosmetic problem for women and men of all ages, regardless of body weight and height. Spider veins are the smallest vessels in and directly beneath the skin, visible as fine red or blue lines.

## How do they develop
The pathological enlargement of the veins is often based on connective tissue weakness and circulatory disorders.

## Contributing factors include
- Jobs involving prolonged standing or sitting

- Being overweight

- Pregnancy

- Family predisposition

## What can we do about it
Before any treatment, we carry out a careful phlebological assessment to determine whether varicose vein disease is present. The examination is painless and free of side effects and is performed using appropriate ultrasound procedures.

## The treatment includes
- Sclerotherapy of the vessels

- Laser therapy

- In the case of larger varicose veins, various surgical

procedures are considered and discussed with you.
