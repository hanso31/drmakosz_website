## What is acne
Acne is one of the most common skin conditions among adolescents, but it can also occur in adulthood. If not treated adequately, scars may remain.

On the face, back and central chest area, the following develop:

- Blackheads (comedones)

- Nodules (spots)

- Pus-filled blisters

## How does it develop
Hormonal changes (particularly during puberty) lead to:

- excessive sebum production

- a keratinisation disorder of the sebaceous gland ducts

- inflammation caused by bacteria

## What can we do about it
Our treatment depends on the severity of the acne and is individually tailored for each patient, combining:

- Detailed advice on proper skincare

- Treatment with medicated creams and tablets

- Professional deep cleansing

- Fruit acid treatment

- Injections

- Laser therapy
