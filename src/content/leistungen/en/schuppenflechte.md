## What is psoriasis
Psoriasis is a fairly common skin disease that usually manifests itself through heavily scaling, reddish skin patches ranging from pinpoint-sized spots to areas as large as the palm of a hand.

## Typical areas of the body affected by psoriasis are
- The tops of the knees

- The outer sides of the elbows

- The navel

- The scalp

Changes to the nails as well as joint complaints can also occur.

## How does it develop
The exact cause is still unclear. The following factors play a role:

- Genetic predisposition

- Immune system disorders

- Medication

- Stress

- Mechanical strain

## What can we do about it
After taking a very detailed medical history, we initiate various examinations:

- Clinical examination of the type of psoriasis

- Blood tests

- Fungal and bacterial tests

- Search for an inflammatory focus

- Joint checks

- X-ray examinations

The therapy is individually adapted to you; we use the following methods:

- Treatments with creams and ointments

- Phototherapy

- Tablet treatments

- Biologics (the newest and highly effective treatment for psoriasis)
