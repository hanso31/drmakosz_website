## What are warts
Warts are various types of skin growths that frequently occur on the hands and feet (verrucas) but also in the genital area (genital warts) and on other parts of the body.

## How do they develop
As warts are caused by various viruses, the development of these changes is always based on an infection. Warts are contagious.

## What can we do about it
The treatment depends on the type of wart. After a thorough examination, we discuss the treatment options with you:

- Application of solutions and ointments

- Removal by laser

- Light therapy

- Excision (surgical removal)
