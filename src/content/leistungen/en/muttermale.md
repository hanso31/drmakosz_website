## What are moles
Moles (nevi) are benign new growths of skin cells and are often congenital. They can vary greatly in shape, colour and size.

## How do they develop
They are often genetically determined and represent a predisposition of the skin. Excessive sun exposure can affect moles and lead to the development of skin cancer.

## What can we do about them
First, we examine the mole very closely with the help of a dermatoscope and computer-assisted dermatoscopy and make the appropriate diagnosis (see also Skin Tumours).

## Possible treatment methods are
- Laser therapy

- Surgical and plastic procedures
