## What is a Vitamin C Drip Spa infusion
Vitamin C is an essential, water-soluble vitamin that fulfils numerous important functions in the human body. Among other things, it contributes to the normal function of the immune system, the protection of cells from oxidative stress and normal collagen formation.

With a Vitamin C Drip Spa infusion, vitamin C is administered intravenously. This means that absorption does not take place via the gastrointestinal tract. The dosage and procedure are individually tailored to your personal circumstances.

## What we can achieve with the Vitamin C Drip Spa infusion
- Support for the normal function of the immune system

- Contribution to the protection of cells from oxidative stress

- Support for normal energy metabolism

- Promotion of normal collagen formation

- Support for a healthy complexion

Before every treatment, an individual consultation takes place. Possible pre-existing conditions, medication and personal needs are taken into account.
