## What skin tumours are there
There are benign and malignant skin growths. Malignant skin growths are also referred to as skin cancer.

## Skin cancer is divided into
- Precancerous lesions: actinic keratoses (carcinoma in situ)

- Non-melanoma skin cancer: basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, Bowen's disease

- Malignant melanoma

## How do they develop
The sun plays a very important role in the development of skin cancer. Frequent sunburn and inadequate sun protection are often the causes.

## What can we do about it
A skin cancer screening should be carried out at least once a year.

A skin examination is performed with the help of a dermatoscope.

This is followed by computer-assisted photographic documentation and evaluation of skin changes and moles.

Depending on the diagnosis, we discuss the treatment options with you:

- Photodynamic therapy

- Laser treatment

- Cryotherapy

- Excision

We also provide you with detailed information about prevention and sun protection measures.
