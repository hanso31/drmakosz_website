Dr. med. Tatiana Makosz

Specialist (FMH) in Dermatology and Venereology

We look forward to your visit and want you to feel at ease with us. We are happy to advise you on all matters relating to the skin, dermatology and beauty.

## Curriculum Vitae

- 2005 Specialist qualification, Ärztekammer Westfalen-Lippe, Germany

- 2001-2004 Dermatology, Universitätsklinik Benjamin Franklin, Charite, Berlin (Prof. Dr. med. C. Orfanos)

- 2004-2006 Dermatology, Klinikum Lippe-Lemgo (teaching hospital of the University of Hannover, Prof. Dr. med. E. Dippel), focus on dermatological oncology and dermatological surgery

- 2007-2010 Senior physician, Bodensee Laserklinik, focus on aesthetic medicine and laser therapy

- 2010-2012 Dermatology practice Dr. med. A. Rozsondai, Radolfzell am Bodensee

- 2012 Practice takeover (Dr. med. FMH A. Gautier), Aeschenvorstadt 21, Basel

**Memberships:**

- Foederatio Medicorum Helveticorum (FMH)

- Swiss Society of Dermatology and Venereology

- European Academy of Dermatology and Venerology

- Arbeitsgemeinschaft Ästhetische Dermatologie und Kosmetologie

- International Dermoscopy Society

- Landesärztekammer Baden-Württemberg

- Laser safety officer for medical applications (Germany)
