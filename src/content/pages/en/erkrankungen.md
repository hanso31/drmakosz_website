As a competent point of contact, we are at your disposal for all questions relating to the skin, its conditions, beauty and aesthetic medicine, as well as in related fields such as allergology, phlebology, proctology and sexually transmitted diseases. Our aim is to advise you competently and thoroughly and to offer you an effective, up-to-date treatment based on sound specialist knowledge that is tailored to your needs. Personal contact with our patients and your trust are very important to us. Of course, we will inform you comprehensively about the various treatment options and their procedures. You decide on the type of treatment yourself. We would be delighted if you placed your trust in us.
